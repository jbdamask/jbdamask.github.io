You have $1M to invest in AI this year, how do you think about:

* Which models to use and when?
* When to use an LLM, when to RAG, when to fine-tune, when to roll our own?
* How important is reproducibility to my use case(s)?
* LLMs will not tell the truth, the whole truth and nothing but the truth. Is this acceptable for my use case(s)?
* Reasoning, planning and execution. How much am I willing to let an AI be responsible for?
* Building AI apps means I need AI software experts. How will I find them? How can I upskill my existing employees?
* How do we avoid spending precious time, money or people on things that seem innovative now, but may be commodity in a few months?
* I'm going to need to build an AI Center of Excellence, but do I need to hire a Chief AI Officer?
* How much to invest and how aggressively?

YMMV
