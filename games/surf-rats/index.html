HOW TO FISH MONTAUK

You are BILL. Walk the shore map and pick a spot, then hit FISH HERE.

ARROWS / WASD move. UP wades out. DOWN comes in. SPACE casts, retrieves, climbs a boulder, or reels.

Rocky spots have boulders. Wade out and climb for better water — waves can shove you back or knock you off.

Wind piles seaweed onto the onshore side. A fouled lure catches nothing. Change type (1–4) and color ([ ]) until something eats. Nothing is guaranteed.

When you hook up, pump the rod with SPACE. Too much heat snaps the line. Too little and a shark or seal may steal the bass. Points count only when you LAND the fish. Then you let it go.

A trip is 6 hours. One real minute is one game hour. Click a map marker to hike the coast (hiking costs time).
