John Damask is a technology executive, software engineer, and AI specialist with over 25 years of experience building systems, teams, and departments that modernize how organizations use technology. He currently runs [Amroja LLC](https://amroja.com) where he develops software products and also serves executive teams as a fractional CTO to lead AI acceleration and navigate the landscape with speed, clarity, and sound governance.

Before forming Amroja, John was a founding member of the technology organization at Flagship Pioneering, a leading biotechnology investment firm based in Cambridge, MA. At Flagship, he established the Digital Science department to assist portfolio companies with cloud computing, bioinformatics, data management, and software engineering. His team created Flagship’s standard Digital Backbone for Biotech, enabling Flagship to rapidly launch new companies that are technologically-mature from day one. He co-led the firm's AI adoption strategy and pioneered a model for portfolio companies to benefit from collective data. Earlier, he spent over a decade at Novartis Institutes for BioMedical Research, culminating as Global Head of Cloud, where he led the organization's transformation to AWS.

Beyond his executive work, John is a hands-on builder obsessed with using AI to shrink the distance between ideas and reality. He recently launched Now I Get It!, an application that translates complex scientific papers into accessible, interactive web pages — built solo in a matter of weeks. He also regularly releases free, open source tools and Claude Code skills born out of his own engineering practice. He writes and shares actively on agentic engineering, Claude Code, and practical AI adoption. John holds a BA in Musicology from New York University.
